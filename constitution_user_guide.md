# Constitution Workspace Module: User Guide

Welcome to the Constitution Workspace Module! This tool acts as the digital parliament for the organization, allowing you to collaboratively draft, review, collate feedback, and publish the organization's constitution through a rigorous, multi-tiered hierarchy.

This guide covers the distinct roles and workflows for **Administrators**, **Officials** (Branch, LGA, State, National), and **Members**.

---

## 1. Administrators

As an Administrator, you initiate the constitution process, manage drafts, and drive the review stages.

### Creating a New Draft
1. Navigate to **Admin Dashboard > Constitution**.
2. Click the **+ Create Draft** button.
3. Provide a **Title** (e.g., "Constitutional Review Draft 2026").
4. You can either type/paste the text directly into the **Content** area OR upload an existing Word Document (`.doc`, `.docx`).
5. Click **Save Draft**.

### Assigning Reviewers
1. Select a draft from the list.
2. Go to the **Reviewers** tab.
3. Use the search bar to find users by name or email.
4. Click the **+** icon next to their name to assign them. They will receive an in-app notification.

### Advancing Review Stages & Scheduling Workshops
The review process flows sequentially: `Branch > LGA > State > National`.
For each level, there is a **Review Phase** followed by a **Harmonisation Workshop Phase**.

1. **Start Review**: Click "Start [Level] Review" (e.g., "Start Branch Review"). This opens the draft to officials at that level and notifies them.
2. **Schedule Workshop**: Once the review period is over, click "Start [Level] Workshop" (e.g., "Start Branch Workshop"). 
   - A dialog will appear asking you to pick a **Date and Time**.
   - The system will automatically create an online meeting in the Meetings module for that exact time and send calendar notifications to all relevant members.
3. **Advance Level**: After the workshop, click "Advance to [Next Level]" (e.g., "Advance to LGA").

### Final Approval
Once the draft passes the National Workshop, click **Final Approve**.
- The system will automatically archive the previous constitution.
- The new constitution becomes active globally.
- An automated email and push notification is sent to *all* registered users announcing the new constitution.

## 2. Officials (Branch, LGA, State, National)

Officials are responsible for reviewing feedback from their subordinates and collating it for the next level up.

### Viewing the Constitution Draft
1. Navigate to **Official Dashboard > Constitution**.
2. Select the active draft. You can read the text or download the original Word document.

### The Collation Dashboard
Once the draft reaches your jurisdiction level (e.g., LGA Review Phase), you must review feedback from the level below you.
1. Navigate to **Official Dashboard > Constitution > Collation**.
2. **Review Grouped Feedback**: 
   - **LGA Officials** will see all feedback submitted by ordinary Members, neatly grouped by their respective **Branches**.
   - **State Officials** will see feedback submitted by LGA Officials, grouped by **LGA**.
   - **National Officials** will see feedback submitted by State Officials, grouped by **State**.
3. **Submit Collation**: On the left side of the dashboard, use the Collation Form to submit your harmonized, finalized feedback on behalf of your jurisdiction. This collation will be passed to the next highest tier.

---

## 3. Ordinary Members

Members are the foundation of the review process. They can provide initial observations on the constitution before it flows up the chain.

### Submitting Feedback
1. Navigate to **Member Dashboard > Constitution**.
2. Select the active draft to read it.
3. Go to the **Feedback** tab.
4. (Optional) Specify the exact section or article number your feedback pertains to.
5. Write your observations, suggestions, or concerns.
6. Click **Send**. Your feedback will automatically be routed to your respective LGA Official for collation during the Branch Review phase.
