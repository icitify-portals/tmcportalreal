The `app/` directory is the Next.js App Router entry point. It composes three top-level concerns: (1) public-facing routes under `(public)/`, `[jurisdiction]/`, and flat pages (`about`, `adhkar`, `competitions`, `programmes`, etc.) rendered by the root `layout.tsx` plus the PWA service worker in `sw.ts`; (2) an authentication flow split across `auth/` UI pages and the `api/auth/[...nextauth]` handler; and (3) a large `dashboard/` subtree gated by role-based layouts for admin, member, and official roles, each with its own nested layout. All client/server data mutations go through `api/` Route Handlers organized by domain (`admin`, `auth`, `chats`, `contests`, `livekit`, `members`, `payments`, `roles`, `settings`, `users`, etc.), which are consumed by both public pages and dashboard pages via shared server actions or fetch calls. Jurisdictional isolation is enforced by the `[jurisdiction]/` segment that wraps campaigns, programmes, and CMS content per tenant.
