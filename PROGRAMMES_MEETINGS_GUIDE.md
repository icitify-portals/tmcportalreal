# TMC Portal: Programmes and Meetings User Guide

Welcome to the updated guide for managing **Programmes** and **Meetings** on the TMC Portal. This guide incorporates all the latest advanced scheduling, notifications, and jurisdiction features designed to make administration seamless across all levels (National, State, Local Government, and Branch).

---

## 1. Creating a Programme or Meeting

When you log into your dashboard (as an Admin or Official), navigate to either the **Programmes** or **Meetings** tab to schedule a new event.

### Setting the Date (Including Past Dates)
- You can schedule an event for a future date.
- **Record-Keeping for Past Events:** If an event has already occurred but was not entered into the system, you can backdate it! The system allows you to create programmes and meetings on past dates provided they fall within the **current calendar year**. This is perfect for maintaining an accurate, complete log of all activities.

### Jurisdiction and Visibility
- **No More Confusion:** Whenever you create a programme, the system automatically tags it with your exact jurisdiction (e.g., *Oyo State*, *Ogun State*, or your specific Branch).
- When a general programme (like *Adhkar* or *Waali Cup*) is submitted for approval or displayed publicly, intending participants and approving admins will clearly see the hosting jurisdiction attached to the card.

---

## 2. Setting Up Meeting Groups (For Meetings Only)

Before creating a Meeting, you must assign it to a **Meeting Group**. Meeting groups allow you to save a reusable list of attendees so you do not have to manually invite people every time.

- **Jurisdiction Filtering:** When you click the "Meeting Group" dropdown while scheduling a meeting, the system will **only drop down groups that belong to your specific jurisdiction**. 
- If the dropdown is empty, you must first go to the **Meeting Groups** tab and click "Create Group" to assemble your attendees.

---

## 3. Advanced Google Calendar-Style Recurring Events

You no longer need a separate, complicated calendar module to handle repetitive events. The system now features a robust recurrence engine.

1. **Selecting Frequency:** In the creation dialog, look for the **Frequency** dropdown.
2. **Standard Options:** You can choose simple rules like `WEEKLY` or `MONTHLY`.
3. **Custom (Google Calendar) Rules:** If you choose `CUSTOM`, you can set complex schedules, such as *"The Second Saturday of every month"* or *"Every Tuesday and Thursday"*.
4. **Auto-Generation:** The system will instantly calculate the dates and auto-generate every single occurrence of that meeting or programme from the start date up until the **end of the year** (up to 52 occurrences). 

---

## 4. Continuous Daily Reminders (3-2-1 Countdown)

You don't need to manually remind your members about upcoming events. The system handles this entirely in the background.

- Every day at **9:00 AM**, the portal's automated scheduler scans the database for upcoming scheduled programmes and meetings.
- If an event is exactly **3 days away**, **2 days away**, or **1 day away**, the system will automatically dispatch continuous reminder notifications and emails to all registered participants and invited attendees. 
- *(Note: This ensures members are well-informed without the admins having to lift a finger!)*

---

### Need Help?
If you encounter any issues creating an event, ensure that you have selected the correct organization in your dashboard (if you hold multiple roles) and that your Meeting Group is properly populated.
